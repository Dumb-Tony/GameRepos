# 10 · Replayability

> **Design law #5:** the second playthrough is a first playthrough. Target: 4–8
> meaningfully distinct full runs before content genuinely repeats.

## Structural replay (built into the branching)

- **Companion exclusivity:** six companions + solo = seven emotional registers, seven
  ability kits, seven ~25-event content layers, seven sets of heart scenes, quests, and
  endings. Nine is *secret* — most players learn she exists from a later run or a friend.
- **Route exclusivity:** Chapter 5 ships in three wholly different variants; the
  Tidewell choice locks out its siblings; ~35% of any run's regions/content is
  unreachable that run by design.
- **Thresholds with teeth:** salvage pairs, base site, the Smoke, Old Grin's Toll,
  Vane's Question, the migration shout — each reshuffles two+ later chapters.
- **The Ledger epilogues:** even repeating an ending reads differently — epilogue cards
  assemble from that run's specific flags.

## Randomized texture (fresh even on the same branch)

Daily weather rolls, drift-loot tables, region encounter tables with anti-repeat
memory, rare events (most players see 2–3 of the 8 per run), bottle messages, and
wildlife population dynamics that respond to the player's hunting/fishing pattern.

## Secrets inventory (discovery fuel)

Nine herself · the Canopy Roads · the Gullet's Heartroom · the sea-gate · the Inner
Green · the courier case's three opening methods · the white boar omen chain · the
seven bottles · X-series endings · the fever-dream lore cycle · Edda's rooster ·
the second black box on Bone Beach · the meteor wish flag · 7 "impossible" almanac
entries (species that shouldn't be on any island — Hum lore in disguise).

## Collectibles (75)

| Set | Count | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Kaari glyph stones | 30 | Full Sundering history; gates X1 |
| Vane's journal pages | 24 | Halcyon/Incident narrative; gates M4 |
| Edda's recipes | 12 | Best cooking, the medicine tier, and her arc's texture |
| The photograph's fragments | 9 | The prologue stranger's story — a quiet, devastating side-tale that recontextualizes the crash |

Plus the 58-species **almanac** (a completion meter that is also the Island Regard
tutorial — you learn the island by attending to it).

## New Game+ — "Driftwood Loops"

Framed diegetically: the island remembers, and eventually, so do you.

- **Carry-over:** the almanac, glyph translations, map annotations, Edda's recipes —
  *knowledge*, not stuff. Plus **one keepsake** (Tier-4 craft) chosen at the prior
  ending, granting one heirloom perk and unique recognition dialogue.
- **Recognition:** NG+ adds déjà-vu dialogue options ("…I know a way through the
  mangroves," you say, and cannot say how you know). Edda notices. Naia *reports it*.
  The Kaari have a word for people like you, and it isn't a comfortable one.
- **NG+-only content:** ending X3 (*The Loop*), the grotto journal, one new Tidewell
  dialogue branch, and the true meaning of the Hum's seven-beat chorus.
- **Run modifiers (unlocked, optional):** Hard Season (monsoon starts Ch4) · Silent
  Island (no companion candidates — enforced solo) · Kind Tide (story mode: meters
  soften) · Chaos Drift (event weights doubled, rare events tripled).

## The Ledger Opens (post-run report)

Every ending unrolls the run's Ledger: choices made, roads not taken (silhouetted, not
spoiled — "Someone else might have trusted the monkey"), endings adjacent to yours, and
the companion-availability reveal. This screen is the replay engine's salesman: it
proves the game remembered, and hints — never states — at how much it still hasn't shown.
