# 09 · Endings (49)

Every ending is an authored scene + epilogue card assembled partly from Ledger flags
(so two players earning the same ending still read different epilogues). Tone tags:
🌅 triumphant · 🍂 bittersweet · 🌑 tragic · 🌀 strange · 😂 joke.

Requirements below are the *primary* gates; companions, Hope band, and alive/dead cast
modify every ending's text.

## Ending distribution logic

Convergence resolves in priority order: death states → active secret/joke chains →
Tidewell commitments (M) → companion-arc finales (C) → route-dominant standard endings
(E/S/M) → fallback (E1/S1 by higher of Signal/Roots). Ties break toward the ending
whose *requirements the player most recently advanced* — the game honors momentum.
