# 06 · Characters

The companions are the co-stars ([03-companions.md](03-companions.md)). These are the
humans, ancestors, and beasts around them.

## The Player — "the Castaway"

Named by the player; background chosen in prologue (Flight Medic / Wildlife Photographer
/ Line Cook / Marine Engineer — see [01-overview.md](01-overview.md)). Written as a
specific person with a hole in their old life (each background's flashback implies why
nobody is waiting too hard back home — which is what makes the Signal/Roots question
honest rather than obvious). The prose voice is second person, present tense.

## Edda Voss 👵 — the hermit

*Botanist of Project Halcyon, 74, the one who stayed.*

- **Role:** mentor, gatekeeper of lore, the game's thorniest relationship. She has
  spent fifty years becoming the island's de-facto caretaker and does not want a
  neighbor, an apprentice, or — heaven forbid — a *rescue*.
- **Personality:** flinty, funny, precise. Feeds you and insults you in the same
  breath. Her tenderness surfaces only sideways — through plants, through Moa, through
  what she doesn't say about the graves behind her grove (two: her colleague-and-love
  Dr. Ilsa Vane, and a rooster).
- **Relationship system:** a trust track like a companion's (Wary → Kindred), moved by
  respect for the island (Island Regard feeds it), honesty, and *not pushing*. She is
  the key to: advanced medicine (marsh fever cure), plant lore, the truth about the
  Incident, and the guardian covenant's human history.
- **Arc:** her health fails across the run (the player can slow this — Flight Medic
  especially — but not stop it). Whether she dies alone, dies accompanied, sails away
  on Ryo's boat under protest, passes her role to you, or — one route — is welcomed at
  last by the Kaari she's protected without ever meeting: that's the player's doing.
  Central to endings S6, S7, M6.
- **Companion chemistry:** despises Ipo (reconciliation questline exists and is
  delightful); respects Kavi; is disarmed by Moa (the rooster grief); tolerates Buri as
  useful livestock (he adores her); has waited *decades* to see Nine's species do what
  Nine does; finds Vela "the only sensible one of you."

## Dr. Ilsa Vane 📓 — the voice in the journals (deceased)

*Halcyon's lead researcher. Died in/after the Incident, 1979. Present through 24 journal
pages, the station's tape reels, and Edda's silences.*

A rigorous, wondering mind slowly realizing her instruments are describing something her
funding agency must never learn — the journals track her turn from *studying* the island
to *protecting* it, ending at the sealed drawer and the Incident. She is the mystery
route's narrator-across-time, and the player's mirror: her question — *report it, or
keep it?* — is the player's Ch6 question, fifty years early. Ending M4 completes her work.

## Ryo Nakata ⛵ — the second survivor

*Solo circumnavigator, 38, reeled in by the Hum in Chapter 4 (conditional arrival).*

- **Role:** ally, dependent, foil — the voice of *leaving*. Competent at sea, hopeless
  ashore (inverting the player's hundred days of hard-won skill: suddenly *you're* the
  local). Arrives injured; how the player manages his recovery sets the relationship.
- **Personality:** warm, restless, allergic to stillness; jokes as deflection. He lost
  his shore-life to the sailing dream and can't admit the dream broke first (the Hum
  caught a man already adrift). Signal-aligned: he *will* rebuild that boat, and he'd
  rather do it with you.
- **Tension design:** Ryo is genuinely good — and still a cost. He eats, he pushes
  escape when the player's route may not, his boat splits the endgame (several endings
  hinge on who sails), and his loyalty must be earned or he'll leave alone (a
  Threshold — with the player's letters, coordinates, or silence aboard).
- **Companion chemistry:** instantly bonds with Buri and Kavi; wages a doomed prank war
  with Ipo; is unsettled by Nine ("that thing *watches* us, mate"); teaches Moa to
  perch on the tiller.

## Naia 🌿 — the watcher in the green

*Kaari scout, early 20s, assigned to observe the castaway. First "contact" is the player
slowly realizing weeks of small oddities — moved snares, a left gift of fruit during
starvation, a glimpse of eyes in the Ch3 storm — were one person.*

- **Role:** the living key to the Inner Green; first contact protocol made personal.
  Her formal contact scene (Ch5C, or Ch6 on other routes) is gated on **Island Regard**
  — the player's accumulated ecological conduct — not on route points: the island's
  people judge how you *lived*, not what you sought.
- **Personality:** curious discipline; raised on stories of what outsiders bring
  (Halcyon is recent memory), fighting her own fascination. Learns the player's
  language faster than she admits; her sense of humor arrives without warning.
- **Arc:** watcher → contact → advocate (she argues the player's case before the Kaari
  elders in Ch6 — the scene assembles evidence *from the Ledger*: every kindness and
  cruelty the island witnessed, testified). Central to M2, M6, and the Perfect ending.
- **Companion chemistry:** the Kaari read companions as the island's own testimony —
  arriving with a Kindred-tier bond is worth more before the elders than anything the
  player can say.

## The Elder Speaker, Tekau 🗿

Leader of the Inner Green's council; appears Ch6+. Old enough to remember Halcyon's
drills. The covenant's living keeper and the hardest audience in the game — Tekau's
questions at the Tidewell are the endgame's moral exam, and they are all questions the
game has already watched the player answer with actions.

## The beasts 🐗🐊

**The Boar King** — a massive, scar-plated old bearded pig, tusks like bad promises;
the inland antagonist. Not evil: *displaced* (Halcyon-era snares took his sounder;
the Ledger eventually shows you his story too). Wrecks camps, contests the Highlands,
raids the Ch5 Homestead. Resolutions: kill (trap/fight — meat, safety, Island Regard
cost), avoid (permanent travel tax), or — Kavi's tracking or Buri's grown challenge —
the non-lethal resolutions that end with the Highlands shared. Kills the unprepared (D7).

**Old Grin** — a six-meter saltwater crocodile older than Edda, sovereign of the
Mangrove Maze. Less an enemy than a *toll* — patient, territorial, horrifyingly fast.
Every route needs the East Passage eventually; every party solves Grin differently
(bait, boat, bridge, Ipo's diversion, Nine's reconnaissance, Vela's warnings — or blood,
D8). Killing Grin is possible, late, costly — and the mangroves' rat population
explosion afterward is a taught consequence.

**Supporting fauna cast:** the monitor lizard (nest-raider, Vela's nemesis), the reef
shark, the macaque troop and its matriarch, Kavi's old pack and the rival male, the
sandbar heron the player can befriend (pure Hope content), and fifty-odd almanac species
([11-worldbuilding.md](11-worldbuilding.md)).
