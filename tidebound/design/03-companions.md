# 03 · The Companion System

> **Design law #2:** the companion is the biggest branch in the game — and a character,
> not a tool. Each has a personality, a fear, a loyalty arc, and things it will never be
> good at. Choosing one changes the mechanics, the story, *and the emotional register*
> of the entire run.

## The Clearing of Eyes (the choice)

During Chapter 1's scarce first hours, each candidate animal gets a distinct,
personality-establishing first encounter (see [02-story.md](02-story.md)). At the Day-3
Threshold, the player commits to **one** courtship — pursuing an animal costs segments,
food, and risk that raw survival desperately wants. The others don't vanish: rejected
candidates recur as wild NPCs all game (Ipo will still rob you; Vela still wheels over
the cliffs; the pack still sings at night), a standing reminder of the road not taken.

**Nine, the octopus, is secret:** her tide-pool encounter is easy to miss, and she only
becomes a candidate if the player returns to the pools twice before Day 3 — most players
discover she *exists* on a later run.

**Choosing no one is a route, not an absence** — see [The Solo Route](#the-solo-route).

## Trust: the universal system

Trust runs 0–100 per companion, in five tiers. It is never shown as a number — only as
**behavior** (distance kept, sleeping position, whether they share food, whether they
come when called).

| Tier | Range | Name | Behavioral signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0–24 | **Wary** | Follows at a distance, flees loud action, won't take food from your hand |
| 1 | 25–49 | **Tolerant** | Shares camp, accepts food, abilities work ~half the time |
| 2 | 50–74 | **Bonded** | Comes when called, abilities reliable, first heart scene unlocked |
| 3 | 75–99 | **Devoted** | Initiates affection, acts unprompted to protect/provide, second heart scene |
| 4 | 100 | **Kindred** | Loyalty-arc finale scene; unlocks the companion's Kindred ability and their exclusive endings |

**Gaining trust (all companions):** feeding (species-preferred foods ×2), keeping
routine (greeting them each Dawn), succeeding together, treating their injuries,
navigating their **fear events** well, and completing their exclusive quests.

**Losing trust (all companions):** neglect (2+ days without interaction), yelling or
striking (massive, some permanent), letting them go hungry while you eat, failing them
in a fear event, and companion-specific betrayals listed below. Below 25, a companion
can **leave** — a devastating scripted scene, and they are gone for the run (their wild
self may still appear).

**Fear events:** each companion has a fear that fires as scripted scenes (and randomly
in matching weather/locations). Handled with patience and preparation, a fear event is
the single biggest trust gain in the game. Mishandled, it's the biggest loss. At
Devoted+, companions begin *facing* their fear for the player's sake — the emotional
core of every loyalty arc.

**Injury & mortality:** companions can be wounded (Boar King, Old Grin, storms, their
own recklessness). Untreated wounds can be fatal. A companion's death mid-run is a
full grief arc — Hope crater, altered dialogue for the rest of the game, a grave you
can tend, and access to endings C2/D-variants. The game never treats it as a stat loss.

## KAVI — the island dog 🐕

*Descendant of a shipwreck's dogs gone wild generations ago; a lean, storm-grey,
dingo-like pariah dog. Cast out of his pack after losing a fight to the new leader.*

- **Personality:** patient, watchful, grave. The oldest soul of the six. Communicates in
  glances and small gravities. Dry comic timing: he judges your worst plans by lying
  down in the middle of them.
- **Fear: fire.** Burn-scarred flank from a lightning fire as a pup. Panics near open
  flame — which is a *daily* problem in a survival game and by design: Kavi's arc forces
  the player to reorganize their whole camp (fire pit away from shelter, windbreaks) or
  slowly, patiently desensitize him. His Devoted fear-scene — stepping between the
  player and an advancing grassfire during the Ch5 lightning storms — is one of the
  game's flagship moments.
- **Loyalty arc — "The Last Pack":** abandonment → cautious partnership → the pack
  returns in Ch5, starving, and his old rival challenges him. The player chooses:
  drive the pack off (safety; Kavi grieves), feed them (`PACK_DEBT`, costly, opens
  ending C1), or let Kavi answer the challenge (risk of injury/death; if he wins, the
  pack is yours in Ch6–7).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Abilities** | Tracks any animal (hunting yields +50%); guards camp at night (blocks theft/predator night events); detects predators before ambushes (converts ambush events into pre-warned choices); can carry small game back |
| **Kindred ability** | *Packmind:* in danger scenes, a third timed-choice option appears — "trust Kavi" — which always has the best outcome |
| **Excels** | Hunting, jungle and highland travel, night security, fighting retreats, finding Ryo in the Ch4 wreck event |
| **Struggles** | Anything vertical (cliffs, canopy), swimming long distances, the reef (can't help at all), stealth around wildlife you *don't* want dead, and every scene involving fire until his arc resolves |
| **Preferred food** | Cooked fish, marrow bones |
| **Betrayal triggers** | Using fire aggressively near him; killing the pack rival by trap instead of letting the challenge happen |
| **Heart scenes** | ① *Scar* — he lets you touch the burn; flashback in dog-dream logic. ② *The Singing* — pack howls at night; he doesn't answer; you realize he's chosen. ③ (Kindred) *Grey Muzzle* — he brings you the pack rival's dropped bone-token: the wild has conceded you're his. |
| **Exclusive quests** | *The Boar King's Trail* (only Kavi can track it to the wallow — enables the non-lethal Boar King resolution); *Old Bones* (dig up the shipwreck dog-collar of his ancestor — lore + his origin) |
| **Exclusive endings** | C1 *The Last Pack*, C2 *Good Boy* (see [09-endings.md](09-endings.md)) |

## IPO — the crab-eating macaque 🐒

*A young male long-tailed macaque, low-ranked in his troop and compensating with crime.
Steals the player's lighter in his very first scene.*

- **Personality:** mischievous, vain, brilliant, exhausting. The comic-relief companion
  who is also, quietly, the loneliest — his troop tolerates him at the margins. He
  doesn't want food from you at first; he wants an *audience*.
- **Fear: the dark.** Caves, the Gullet, the temple's inner passages, moonless nights.
  He shrieks, clings, or bolts — and a bolting monkey in a cave is a lost monkey. His
  Devoted fear-scene: holding a burning brand in his own small hands, teeth chattering,
  walking into the Gullet ahead of you because you're injured and someone has to.
- **Loyalty arc — "Audience of One":** performer → partner-in-crime → the Ch5 choice:
  his troop's new matriarch offers him a real place at last — status he always wanted —
  *if* he abandons the weird hairless thing. Whether he stays is a direct function of
  Trust and whether you ever laughed at (vs. with) him in earlier scene choices.
- **Trouble mechanic (unique):** Ipo generates *incidents* — he steals from Edda (she
  will threaten to shoot him; a whole reconciliation questline exists), triggers ruin
  wards, provokes the Boar King once. Every incident is also an opportunity; Ipo's route
  is chaos surfing, and Roots-focused players will feel real friction. Advantages and
  disadvantages, by design.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Abilities** | Canopy travel (unlocks all treetop paths & the Canopy Roads region); retrieves fruit/eggs/items from unreachable spots; nimble fingers (opens latched/jammed containers — including two Halcyon lockers no one else can); pickpockets… and gifts you what he steals |
| **Kindred ability** | *Second Pair of Hands:* one extra minor action per Day segment (he forages/fetches autonomously) |
| **Excels** | Jungle & ruins exploration, fruit economy (you will never starve for fruit), puzzle spaces, distracting Old Grin, morale (Hope regen aura when Bonded+) |
| **Struggles** | Open water (won't board the raft until Devoted), cold rain (health risk in Ch5 — needs a crafted cover), any plan requiring quiet patience, guarding anything (he *is* the theft event), combat (bites, then flees) |
| **Preferred food** | Crabs, figs, anything stolen |
| **Betrayal triggers** | Caging/tethering him after an incident; trading him away to Ryo's "he'd fetch a fortune" joke offer (yes, the game lets you, and no, it does not go how you hope) |
| **Heart scenes** | ① *The Lighter* — he returns it, unprompted, spinning it like a trophy: first true gift. ② *Grooming* — he grooms your hair with total gravity; the prose lets the player realize they've been adopted. ③ (Kindred) *Matriarch's Offer* — the troop scene; he throws the fig back. |
| **Exclusive quests** | *The Heist of Station Halcyon* (a three-stage caper for the sealed archive keys); *Troop Politics* (raise his status via engineered "finds") |
| **Exclusive endings** | C3 *The Trickster's Crown*, plus unique variant of E2 |

## VELA — the white-bellied sea eagle 🦅

*A huge female sea eagle with one pale, storm-blind eye. Nests on Kestrel Cliffs.
Her first act is dropping a half-eaten fish at the player's feet — payment, it turns
out, for the player unknowingly scaring off an egg-thieving monitor lizard.*

- **Personality:** independent, imperious, transactional — the hardest companion to win
  and the only one who starts the relationship as *equals negotiating*. She is not a
  pet and the writing never lets her become one. Affection, when it finally comes, is
  seismic precisely because it's rationed.
- **Fear: storms.** Her blind eye came from the cyclone that killed her first mate. At
  barometric drops she becomes erratic, then vanishes to a hidden roost — leaving the
  player without their scout exactly when weather danger peaks (a deliberate cruelty of
  design: her ability blackout is synchronized with the game's hardest survival windows).
  Devoted fear-scene: she stays through a Ch5 cyclone, shaking, wings mantled over the
  player's food cache like it's a nest.
- **Loyalty arc — "The Wind Takes Nothing Back":** transaction → alliance → the Ch6
  migration: sea eagles don't migrate, but the Hum's Ch6 disturbance drives every bird
  off the island in one unforgettable dusk scene — and she circles, once, to see if the
  player releases her. What the player shouts (or doesn't) at that moment is one of the
  game's most consequential single choices (gates C4 and modifies five endings).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Abilities** | Aerial scouting (reveals region maps before you travel; previews weather one extra day); spots resources/wrecks/drift loot from altitude (beach drift table doubles); danger cries (converts ambushes into warnings anywhere outdoors); carries small items to/from marked points — including across the Gullet chasm |
| **Kindred ability** | *Farsight:* once per chapter, a full island overflight cutscene — reveals one secret the player hasn't found (drives completionism) |
| **Excels** | Navigation (immune to getting lost outdoors), long-range planning, finding Ryo's boat/flare events early, cliff & highland content, fishing the shallows (she strikes fish for you at Bonded+) |
| **Struggles** | Caves and interiors (blackout — cannot enter the Gullet, Halcyon, temple inner halls), dense canopy, all storm content until arc resolves, night (she sleeps — no night protection at all), and she *cannot be hugged* until the very end of her arc, which the game makes you feel |
| **Preferred food** | Fresh fish (whole, un-gutted — she's particular) |
| **Betrayal triggers** | Taking eggs from any raptor nest; caging her during the storm (it works, mechanically — and drops her to Wary) |
| **Heart scenes** | ① *Payment* — she finally eats from your hand, glaring, like she's doing you a favor. ② *The Blind Side* — she starts walking on your left, giving you her good eye: the prose lets attentive players notice before it's said. ③ (Kindred) *Mantling* — the cyclone scene. |
| **Exclusive quests** | *The High Nests* (rescue her late clutch from the monitor lizard — the egg becomes a hatchling subplot); *Chart the Unseen* (aerial mapping of all 17 regions — unlocks the true map collectible) |
| **Exclusive endings** | C4 *The Wind Takes Her*, plus unique variants of E3 (she carries the flare) and M1 |

## BURI — the bearded pig 🐗

*A young bearded pig (island-native wild pig), absurdly strong, bottomless appetite,
zero self-preservation instinct. First encounter: he demolishes the player's Day-1 camp
eating everything, then falls asleep in the wreckage without a flicker of guilt.*

- **Personality:** exuberant, gluttonous, fiercely loving, catastrophically reckless.
  The easiest companion to befriend (food = love) and the hardest to keep alive. Tank
  and toddler in one body. Comic engine of any run he's in — until the moment his
  recklessness stops being funny.
- **Fear: being alone.** No dramatic phobia — Buri fears *abandonment*. Separated from
  the player (script or choice), he panics destructively: breaks pens, tramples the
  farm, charges into danger looking for you. Leaving him behind "for his own good" is
  always mechanically tempting and always emotionally expensive. Devoted fear-scene:
  after his Ch5 injury (below), the player must leave him to fetch medicine — the scene
  where he *chooses to stay put*, shaking, trusting you'll come back, is his arc's hinge.
- **Loyalty arc — "The Shield That Learns":** freeloader → guardian → the Ch5 Homestead
  crisis: the starving Boar King raids, and Buri — massively outmatched — charges it
  anyway to protect the player. He is gravely wounded (scripted; survivable). The rest
  of his arc is the player teaching restraint (or failing to), and it directly sets
  whether he survives the endgame's dangers.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Abilities** | Pack animal (carry capacity ×2 — transforms the resource game); demolition (breaks log-jams, brush walls, Halcyon's rusted doors, the ruin rubble — his own set of gated shortcuts); digging (tubers, truffles, buried caches, the pirate cache); real combat power (the only companion who can *win* a straight fight) |
| **Kindred ability** | *Bulwark:* in any danger scene, Buri interposes — the player cannot take major injury while he's healthy (the damage risk moves to him, which is the point) |
| **Excels** | Base logistics, farming (plows!), swamp and lowland travel (strong swimmer), fighting off predators, the entire Homestead variant of Ch5, keeping Hope up (he is impossible to be sad around) |
| **Struggles** | Cliffs and narrow ledges (hard-blocked from Kestrel Cliffs & high caldera paths), stealth (comically), canopy/reef content, rationing (he *will* eat unsecured stores — forces granary crafting), fine tasks |
| **Preferred food** | Everything; truffles for trust bonus |
| **Betrayal triggers** | Penning him for days without visits; using him as bait; (dark path) the late-game starvation scene offers a choice the game will let you make and never lets you forget |
| **Heart scenes** | ① *Restitution* — he digs up a truffle cache and pointedly deposits it where your rations were: pig apology. ② *The Wall* — first time he plants himself between you and the treeline at a night noise. ③ (Kindred) *Stay* — the medicine scene. |
| **Exclusive quests** | *The Root Cellar* (excavate the collapsed Kaari cellar — major food security + lore); *King of Nothing* (the non-lethal Boar King ending: Buri, fully grown by Ch6, faces him — dominance without death) |
| **Exclusive endings** | C5 *Sounder*, plus unique variants of S1/S3 (he founds a domesticated line) |

## MOA — the red junglefowl 🐔

*A junglefowl hen — small, copper-and-flame feathers, permanently alarmed. The comic
first impression (she is, functionally, a wild chicken) is a trap the design springs on
purpose: Moa's route is the game's most beloved emotional arc in playtests-on-paper.*

- **Personality:** anxious, vigilant, secretly indomitable. Terrified of everything and
  endures anyway. Her route is about what courage actually is. Players who pick "the
  chicken" as a joke get the sincerest story in the game.
- **Fear: storms** — the user-suggested arc, made central. Thunder sends her into blind
  panic: she bolts, hides, or freezes in the open (a death sentence in a monsoon
  chapter). Below Bonded trust, every storm is a search-and-rescue minigame for a
  handful of feathers. With trust built — and a crafted storm-shelter nest box — she
  weathers it trembling against your chest. Her Devoted fear-scene inverts the whole
  dynamic: mid-cyclone in Ch5, *the player* is knocked unconscious outside, and Moa
  leaves cover — into everything she fears — to peck and shriek Edda/Ryo/the companion-
  less camp awake to find you. Storm-terror becomes the route's badge of honor.
- **Loyalty arc — "Brave Feather":** prey-animal panic → routine → the storm inversion
  above → in Ch6, she walks into the Tidewell temple's booming acoustic dark (storm-like
  sound) at your side, feathers flat, without breaking. Small body. Biggest arc.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Abilities** | Eggs (steady daily protein — quietly one of the strongest survival abilities in the game); weather sense (predicts storms/rain a full day out — the *only* forecast without Vela or Halcyon's barometer); dowsing instinct (leads to fresh water sources); alarm calls (wakes you before night threats); her scratching finds seeds & grubs (farming boost) |
| **Kindred ability** | *Stormcall:* her behavior forecasts not just weather but **events** — she reacts a scene early to volcano tremors, Boar King raids, and the Ch6 bird-exodus, giving the player prep time nothing else grants |
| **Excels** | Food stability, water discovery, early-warning defense, farming synergy, Edda's heart (their friendship is its own micro-arc), the entire Hope economy (tending her is a Hope action) |
| **Struggles** | Everything physical: no combat, no carrying, no travel help; predators actively target her (hawk/monitor events exist *because* she's with you — a real cost); storms until the arc turns; long expeditions (she must ride in a carried basket you have to craft) |
| **Preferred food** | Grubs, rice from the Halcyon stores |
| **Betrayal triggers** | Eating her eggs in front of her is fine (she's a chicken, the game is honest about this) — but the one scripted moment where eating *her* is offered as a starvation option is the route's moral cliff, and the Ledger flag is named `YOU_KNOW_WHAT_YOU_DID` |
| **Heart scenes** | ① *Lap* — she simply gets in it, one dusk, and sleeps: claim staked. ② *The Gift* — she lays her egg in your hand-shelter instead of her nest: trust, chicken-theology edition. ③ (Kindred) *Brave Feather* — the cyclone rescue, from her POV, in the game's only non-player-perspective scene. |
| **Exclusive quests** | *The Flock* (gather the island's scattered feral junglefowl — founds a coop, transforms the food game); *Edda's Rooster* (the hermit's decades-old grief has feathers; Moa unlocks it) |
| **Exclusive endings** | C6 *The Rooster's Dawn*, plus the game's most distinct variant of S5 |

## NINE — the day octopus 🐙 (secret companion)

*A reef octopus in the tide pools of Palm Shallows. Found only by players who linger:
she must be encountered twice before Day 3 to become a candidate. Named for what Edda
says when she finally sees her: "You know they've got nine brains? More than the two of
you put together."*

- **Personality:** alien, curious, playful, unsentimental — the writing resists
  mammal-izing her. She solves, tastes, tests. Affection expresses as *attention*: she
  watches you with one slotted eye and copies what you do. The route's tone is quieter,
  stranger, and more wondrous than any other — and shadowed from the start for players
  who know octopus lifespans. The game knows you know. That's the route.
- **Fear:** none, in the phobia sense — her "fear event" analog is **drying**: she is
  bound to water, and every scene of her crossing land (she will, for you, in a wet
  pouch you craft) is a countdown. Her equivalent of the Devoted fear-scene: crossing
  400 meters of storm-wrecked beach overland — slow, dimming — to reach you at the
  lagoon when the tide gate traps you in Ch5.
- **Loyalty arc — "Three Springs":** curiosity → collaboration → senescence. Nine's arc
  is the only one on a biological clock the player cannot fix, and the design commits:
  in long runs she begins to fade in Ch7 regardless of trust. Her exclusive endings are
  about what her attention *meant*. (One secret: the Tidewell's waters are older than
  biology, and the island repays its debts — see ending C8.)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Abilities** | The entire underwater game: retrieves from the shipwreck, the reef, the drowned Halcyon radio room, the temple's sea-gate; opens/unscrews/unknots anything (jars, hatches, the courier case!); camouflage scouting of Old Grin; tool use (she *learns* three tricks the player teaches — a unique mini-system) |
| **Kindred ability** | *Ninefold:* she solves one puzzle-lock per chapter autonomously, including two the player cannot otherwise open |
| **Excels** | Reef, wreck, lagoon, and every drowned space; the mystery route (she is drawn to heartglass — living dowsing rod for the Deep); food from the sea; the courier case secret |
| **Struggles** | Everything above the tideline (land travel = escort scenes with real stakes); cannot help with hunting, farming, fire, storms, or camp defense at all; long inland chapters reduce her to check-in visits (absence is a designed cost); short lifespan shadows Ch7 |
| **Preferred food** | Crabs (live — she wants the hunt) |
| **Betrayal triggers** | Keeping her in the holding pool "for safety" more than a day; showing her location to Ryo before his loyalty is settled (sailors have opinions about octopus as food) |
| **Heart scenes** | ① *Mimic* — she copies your fire-tending gesture with a piece of kelp, and you understand she's been studying you. ② *The Gift Horizon* — she brings up the photograph from the sunken fuselage: the stranger's photo from the prologue, closing that loop only on her route. ③ (Kindred) *Ink* — she writes — once, unmistakably, in the sand at your feet — the spiral glyph from the temple. First proof the island's mystery and its wildlife are connected. |
| **Exclusive quests** | *The Drowned Door* (only Nine can thread the temple sea-gate's passage); *Eight Arms, Three Tricks* (teach her tools — player-chosen, with consequences) |
| **Exclusive endings** | C7 *Nine's Garden*, C8 *Three Springs*, plus the only non-Depth access to M5 |

## The Solo Route 🧍

Choosing no companion (or losing/never pursuing one) is a designed route, not a failure
state. The island reads differently alone: more prose interiority, a harsher survival
tune (no ability crutches — but also no one else to feed, protect, or grieve), unique
scenes (the volleyball— sorry, *coconut* — see ending J3; the long silences; talking to
the Ledger), and Edda/Ryo/Naia relationships that run deeper because they're all you
have. Exclusive ending X4 *Alone, Unbroken* is among the hardest in the game.

| | Kavi 🐕 | Ipo 🐒 | Vela 🦅 | Buri 🐗 | Moa 🐔 | Nine 🐙 |

| Survival power | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★ |
| Exploration unlocks | ★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★ | ★★★★★ |
| Mystery access | ★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★ | ★★ | ★★★★★ |
| Danger protection | ★★★★★ | ★★ | ★★★ | ★★★★★ | ★★★ | ★ |
| Emotional difficulty | ★★★ | ★★ | ★★★★ | ★★★ | ★★ | ★★★★★ |
| Trust difficulty | Medium | Low (keeping it is hard) | Very high | Very low | Low | Medium (finding her is hard) |
| Route affinity | Any | Mystery/chaos | Escape | Settler | Settler | Mystery |

No column wins. That's the point.
