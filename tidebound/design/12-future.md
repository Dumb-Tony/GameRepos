# 12 · Future Expansion Ideas

Post-launch directions, in rough priority order. None are promised by the base design;
all have hooks already planted.

1. **The Other Signal (expansion):** ending X5's sister island — a second, colder
   Vessakai in another ocean, another castaway's story crossing yours by radio. New
   biome, 2–3 new companions (fur seal, raven, arctic fox), the Hum network's larger
   pattern. The big one.
2. **Companion pack:** monitor lizard (villain-turned-companion redemption route),
   fruit bat (night-exploration kit — inverts the day/segment economy), and dolphin
   (open-water traversal; pairs devastatingly with the escape route).
3. **The Halcyon Tapes:** prequel episode playable as Dr. Vane, 1978–79, ending at the
   Incident. The player already knows how it ends; that's the genre. Reuses the island
   map fifty years younger.
4. **Naia's Interlude:** the base game's Ch1–5, retold as the watcher's reports —
   short, cheap to produce against existing scenes, enormous fan payoff.
5. **Seasonal drift events:** dated live-ish content via the drift-loot table (things
   wash ashore; some of them have stories).
6. **Endless Isle mode:** post-ending sandbox on your run's island state — the
   homestead simulator the Roots audience will ask for.
7. **Accessibility & localization passes:** full text-size/contrast/timed-choice-off
   options at launch; localization architecture (string tables, no baked-in text) from
   day one so this list stays possible.
8. **Community almanac:** shareable Ledger cards (endings, almanac completion, joke
   ending badges) as PNG exports — zero-backend virality suited to a static-host game.
