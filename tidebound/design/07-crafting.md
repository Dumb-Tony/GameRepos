# 07 · Crafting & Resources

## Philosophy

Crafting is the survival game's verb and the story's clock: each tier unlock is a
milestone scene (first fire, first roof, first forged blade) and Hope event. Recipes are
discovered by experiment, by lore (Edda, Halcyon manuals, Kaari glyphs), or by
companion accident (Ipo *invents* two recipes by stealing components). No recipe is
missable-forever; some are route/companion-accelerated.

## Resource catalog (44)

**Gathered:** palm frond · coconut · bamboo · vine/cordage fiber · driftwood · hardwood ·
softwood · stone · flint · clay · sand · shell · beeswax · resin/sap · medicinal herbs
(5 kinds: feverbark, aloe-analog, bittergreen, marshmint, blood-moss) · edible plants
(taro, yam, banana, fig, sugarcane, breadfruit, seagrape) · grubs · bird eggs

**Harvested/hunted:** fish (reef/river/deep) · crab · shellfish · small game · boar meat ·
hide · sinew · bone · feathers · fat/tallow

**Salvage:** tarp · rope · sheet metal · wire · glass · nails/fasteners · engine parts ·
fuel · cloth · flare · Halcyon electronics · Halcyon chemicals · rice/seed stock

**Rare/story:** heartglass shard · Kaari tools (obsidian) · pearl · the courier case
contents · black box components

## Recipe tree (by tier)

### Tier 0 — Bare hands (Ch1)
sharp stone · cordage · ember pouch · fire (friction) · lean-to · water carrier (bamboo)
· hand spear · gathering bag · torch

### Tier 1 — Camp (Ch1–2)
stone axe/knife · fishing spear · rain catch · drying rack · clay pot (→ boiling!) ·
bed · fish trap · snare · poultice · splint · signal fire ring · Moa's nest box

### Tier 2 — Homestead (Ch3–4)
hut → fortified hut · smoker · granary (Buri-proof) · bow & arrows · fishing rod ·
machete (salvage edge) · water filter · garden plot · mosquito netting (marsh fever
defense!) · companion gear (Kavi's cool-side sled harness · Ipo's rain cover · Moa's
travel basket · Buri's saddlebags · Nine's wet-pouch — each a small trust scene) ·
canoe/raft Mk I · climbing rope · trail markers

### Tier 3 — Mastery (Ch5+)
kiln → forge (metal edge tools) · loom (cloth) · rice paddy · irrigation · watchtower ·
storm shutters · large raft / boat repair (Ryo synergies) · signal pyre network ·
mirror heliograph · Halcyon generator repair · radio rebuild (the Signal route's grail:
parts from 4 regions + fuel + antenna mast at Kestrel Cliffs) · antivenom (Edda) ·
fever cure (feverbark grove) · diving weights & goggle (glass + sap) — the no-Nine
path into drowned spaces

### Tier 4 — Story crafts (route-gated)
the pyre-crown (Signal finale) · the covenant lamp (Depth finale, heartglass) · the
feast table (Roots finale) · Vane's completed instrument (M4) · the grave goods (grief
arcs) · the keepsake (New Game+ carry item — player chooses what it is)

## Economy tuning targets

- Nothing above Tier 1 is craftable in a single day — big builds take multi-day
  commitments the story can interrupt (storms mid-hut are authored content).
- Salvage is finite; biologicals renew (seasonally, and only if not stripped — regional
  depletion is tracked and feeds Island Regard).
- Every companion warps the economy differently (Buri = logistics ×2, Ipo = fruit/theft
  income, Moa = protein floor, Vela = drift-loot doubling, Nine = salvage access,
  Kavi = meat) — a run's "build order" differs by companion, which is replay texture.
