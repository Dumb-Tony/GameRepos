# 05 · The Island — Exploration & Locations

## Shape of the place

A massive volcanic island (~30 km across) the Kaari called **Vessakai** — *"the mother
who waits."* Broad reef-fringed south coast (crash site), rising through jungle to the
central caldera of **Mother Ash**, with a drowned, storm-eaten north shore. Travel is
by region graph, not open-world grid: moving between adjacent regions costs segments
and rolls encounter tables (see [08-events.md](08-events.md)).

(homestead within the Green Deep), and the drift-changing sandbar islets (random events).

## Gating philosophy

Roughly 14 of 17 regions are partially or wholly gated by **choices** (thresholds,
route points, Island Regard) or **companion abilities** — the map itself is a branching
structure. Every companion has: at least one region only they open easily, and at least
one they cannot enter at all. Solo players can eventually open most doors through
crafting and grit, but always later and at higher cost — thoroughness buys what
companionship grants.

**One-run coverage target: ~65%.** No single run can see everything (Canopy Roads +
sea-gate + Kindred content cannot co-exist), which is load-bearing for replayability.

## Region detail highlights (authoring priorities)

- **The Gullet** is the mystery route's dungeon: three chambers (the Throat, the
  Gallery of Hands — Kaari paintings — and the Heartroom) with water-level puzzles
  keyed to tides. Ipo's dark-fear arc and Nine's finest hour both live here.
- **Station Halcyon** is the game's readable-lore mother lode: 24 journal pages, the
  Incident tape, the sealed wing. It should feel like *Roadside Picnic* domesticated —
  breakfast still on the table, 50 years of dust.
- **Tidewell Temple** is the aesthetic centerpiece: half its nave is tidal — floor
  glyphs legible only at low tide, the sea-gate breathing below. All three routes
  converge here in Ch6 with entirely different scene framing.
- **The Inner Green** must feel *earned*: a caldera valley of terraced gardens and
  tree-woven halls, reached only through regard the player built without knowing it
  was being measured.
