# 08 · Events

Events are the connective tissue between the survival sim and the VN. Four families:
**scripted** (chapter spines & thresholds — see [02-story.md](02-story.md)),
**conditional** (Ledger-triggered), **random** (region/season tables), and **rare**
(low-probability wonders that make runs feel personal).

## Conditional events (Ledger-triggered) — selection

| Event | Trigger | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| *The Second Survivor* | Signal infrastructure ≥2 by Ch4 | Ryo arrives (whole character comes online) |
| *The Pack Returns* | Kavi route + Ch5 | Loyalty arc climax |
| *Edda's Fall* | Edda trust ≥ Bonded + Ch5 | Her illness becomes the player's problem/choice |
| *The Watcher's Gift* | Starvation state + Island Regard ≥ threshold | Naia's anonymous fruit — foreshadows contact |
| *Boar King's Raid* | Any food stores > threshold, unfortified | The antagonist escalates |
| *The Cold Week* | Fire lost in monsoon | Multi-day crisis chain |
| *Grave Visits* | Any death (companion/Edda) | Recurring grief/Hope scenes; the game does not move on quickly |
| *The Courier Case* | Salvaged in prologue OR Nine retrieves it | Slow-burn: locked → opened (three methods) → contents change two endings (X2) |
| *Rat Tide* | Old Grin killed | Delayed ecological consequence chain |
| *Fever Dreams* | Any major disease | Lore-bearing delirium scenes (island memory glimpses) |

## Random event tables (per region × season, weighted)

**Beach/lagoon:** drift loot (weighted table — Vela doubles) · turtle tracks · squall ·
jellyfish bloom (fishing lockout) · footprints that are yours (or aren't) ·
message-in-a-bottle (7 authored bottles, one is a joke, one matters)

**Jungle:** fruit windfall · snake encounter (timed choice) · troop mischief · lost!
(navigation event; Kavi/markers negate) · hidden glyph stone · orchid find (Edda gift
item) · boar sign (Boar King proximity warning)

**Mangrove:** crab surge · Grin sighting (dread, no damage — his patience is the horror)
· leech day (small health/Hope tax) · medicinal moss find

**Highlands/cliffs:** raptor sky-dance (Hope) · rockfall (timed) · seabird egg harvest
window · the Vantage clears (map reveal moment)

**Reef (with access):** pearl find · shark shadow (abort/press choice) · Nine's cousin
(wild octopus cameo; on Nine's route, a scene) · the wreck shifts (new salvage room)

**Night (any):** predator probe (defense stack test) · companion dream-twitch scenes ·
the Hum's chorus (Depth+1, Hope roll) · bioluminescent tide (Hope++, unlocks one Nine
scene) · distant engine sound — or is it? (Signal players lose sleep)

## Rare events (≤5% rolls; most players see 2–3 per run)

the green flash sunset (Hope surge + Edda story unlock) · whale migration seen from
the cliffs · sea-turtle mass hatching (protect-the-nest minigame; big Island Regard) ·
meteor storm (wish scene — flags a secret) · Kaari ghost-lights on the Terrace (Depth
hint) · the white boar (Boar King lore omen) · a plane, high and indifferent, its
contrail dissolving (devastating Signal-route scene; Hope crash or resolve — player's
choice frames it) · Ipo's troop performs the player's daily routine back at them
(pure comedy, secretly a trust metric)

## Weather & disaster events

Squall chains, the Ch5 scripted **cyclone** (shelter-tier exam + every companion's
storm content), lightning fires (Kavi's crucible), flood surge in the Gullet, the Ch6
scripted **tremor ladder** (volcano stirring — intensity scales with heartglass
damage), king tide (drowns/reveals coastal content on a lunar schedule).

## Companion event layers

Each companion adds ~25 bespoke events across the run: trust beats, fear events, ability
moments, incidents (Ipo's crime wave, Buri's appetite, Moa's predator magnetism, Vela's
absences, Nine's escort scenes, Kavi's pack politics). These layers are exclusive by
definition — the single largest chunk of replay-fresh content.

## Event design rules

1. Random events may threaten resources, never instakill — deaths always come from
   *choices* (pressing on, diving deeper, ignoring the forecast).
2. Every timed choice has a safe-but-costly default for players who freeze.
3. Rare events must never gate endings — they flavor runs, they don't decide them
   (exception: joke endings may require rare-event chains; they're jokes).
4. Weighted tables re-roll against recent history (no event repeats within 5 days) so
   runs stay textured.
